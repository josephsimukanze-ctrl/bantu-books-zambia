Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Register" button in the top right corner and fill in your details. You'll need a valid email address and phone number.

Is there a free plan?

Yes! Basic (Mumbi) accounts get 20 free downloads per day. This includes access to older ECZ papers, public domain novels, and basic educational materials.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Airtel Money, MTN Mobile Money, Zamtel Kwacha, and major credit/debit cards.

Can I download books for offline reading?

Yes! All PDF and EPUB files can be downloaded and accessed offline on your device.

How do I upgrade to Premium?

Go to your Profile page and click on "Upgrade Account". Choose your preferred plan and complete the payment.
