About Bantu Books Zambia

Our Mission

To make quality educational and leisure content accessible to every Zambian—from rural schools to urban universities—using free, low-cost, and paid models.

Our Vision

A digitally empowered Zambia where every citizen has access to knowledge in their preferred language and format.

What We Offer

  • ECZ exam papers for Grades 7, 9, and 12 (free and paid)
  • University and college materials from UNZA, CBU, and other institutions
  • Zambian novels and literature from local authors
  • Books in 7 local languages (Bemba, Nyanja, Tonga, Lozi, Kaonde, Luvale, Lunda)
  • Newspaper archives including Times of Zambia and Daily Mail
  • ZNBC educational radio and TV transcripts
